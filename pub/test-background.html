3D Typographic Background Test

This page demonstrates the 3D spinning typographic symbols with gravity physics. The symbols should be floating and falling in the background with realistic physics simulation.

The animation features:

  • 30 randomly placed typographic symbols
  • 3D rotation on X, Y, and Z axes
  • Gravity formula (F = ma) applied to each symbol
  • Realistic bouncing on boundaries
  • Air resistance and friction
  • Variable opacity based on rotation angle

The symbols include: § ¶ ※ ❧ ⸙ ⁂ № ❡ ⌕ © ® @ # & * ~ ^ = + - × ÷ ∞ ∑ ∫ α β γ δ

Technical Implementation:
  • Canvas-based animation with requestAnimationFrame
  • Physics: gravity acceleration, velocity, friction, and bounce damping
  • 3D rotation simulation using transformation matrices
  • Performance optimized with fixed time step
